ESP32 CTF

Connect your board

This page talks to your ESP32 over its USB cable. The board checks your flags and keeps your points.

  1. 1
    Flash the CTF firmware Once per board. Skip it if the board already shows the ESP32 CTF banner. Flash in the browser
  2. 2
    Plug it into this computer Use a USB data cable. If the board doesn't show up in the next step, the cable may only carry power.
  3. 3
    Choose the board's port Your browser lists serial ports. The board usually appears as USB Single Serial or CP2102.

    Reading the board…

    Connected to ESP32-CTF-3456. You start with 100 points.

    Start playing

Works in desktop Chrome and Edge. In Firefox, Safari or on a phone, use a serial terminal at 115200 baud and the player guides instead.
