U1 · flash

ESP32 Multi-Track CTF

Flash one classic ESP32 DevKit and play 40 capture-the-flag challenges across radio, web, serial, hardware pins, firmware reversing and crypto, scored on the board itself.

Your browser has no Web Serial. Use desktop Chrome or Edge, or the esptool command below. Web Serial needs this page to be served over HTTPS (or localhost). Classic ESP32 · 4 MB flash · v0.1.0

Browser flashing uses Web Serial, which works in desktop Chrome and Edge (not Firefox, Safari or mobile browsers). Plug the board in over USB, click the button and pick its serial port.

Or flash from a terminal with esptool (pipx install esptool), with the image from this page:

esptool --chip esp32 -p /dev/ttyUSB0 write-flash 0x0 esp32-ctf-v0.1.0.bin

The port may be /dev/ttyACM0, /dev/cu.usbserial-* or COM3. Download: esp32-ctf-v0.1.0.bin. Flashing again wipes your progress.

U2 · tracks

What's inside

Seven tracks, 40 challenges. Easy 100, medium 200, hard 300 points. The radio tracks live in mode radio (default), the web track in mode net; the rest are always on.

BLE · radio mode

Bluetooth Low Energy

11 challenges · 2000 pts · GATT, advertising

Read, subscribe, write, pair and script your way through a custom GATT service.

BTC · radio mode

Bluetooth Classic

3 challenges · 500 pts · inquiry, SPP

A serial console over Bluetooth and a device that says more than its name.

WEB · net mode

Wi-Fi and web

9 challenges · 1500 pts · SoftAP, HTTP, WebSocket, mDNS, UDP

The board's own access point and web server, from page source to tokens and closed ports.

SER · always on

Serial

4 challenges · 800 pts · USB console, UART2

Boot logs, a second UART, a debug login and a console with secrets.

HW · always on

Hardware pins

6 challenges · 1000 pts · LED, GPIO, touch pads

Blinking lights, buses on the wire, a jumper and a touch keypad.

RE · always on

Firmware reversing

4 challenges · 900 pts · flash dump, console

Dump the flash, parse what is stored there and take the firmware apart in Ghidra.

CRY · always on

Crypto

3 challenges · 600 pts · console

A classic cipher with a scattered key, an encryption oracle and a reused nonce.

Max 7300 points · 6400 without the optional kit · hints cost 25% of a challenge's points

U3 · first steps

First steps

  1. Flash the board

    Use the button above in desktop Chrome or Edge, or the esptool command. The image goes at offset 0x0.

  2. Open the serial console

    115200 baud, 8N1. Press EN/RST on the board to see the boot banner, then type help.

    tio -b 115200 /dev/ttyUSB0

  3. Check the board, pick a track

    status shows your score and mode, list (or list BLE, list HW, …) the challenges. Submit with submit ESP{…}; when stuck, hint <id> shows the cost and hint <id> yes reveals it. mode net switches to the web track.

J1 · optional kit

Optional kit

Five challenges (900 points) need extra hardware. Skip them if you don't have it: everything else needs only a laptop and a phone, and status shows your maximum with and without kit.

  • SER-02USB-UART adapter, 3.3 V logic
  • HW-01one jumper wire
  • HW-03 · HW-04 · HW-05logic analyzer (a cheap sigrok-compatible 8-channel one is enough)
U5 · scope

Every challenge targets the board and nothing else. The board runs its own access point and Bluetooth services; no challenge asks you to scan, join or affect any other network or device. Keep your tools pointed at your own board.
