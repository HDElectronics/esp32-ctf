ESP32 CTF

Set up your board

This page is your guide and scoreboard. It never talks to the board: you play with your own tools and check your flags here.

  1. 1
    Flash the CTF firmware Once per board. Skip it if the board already shows the ESP32 CTF banner. Flash in the browser
  2. 2
    Open a serial terminal and press EN At 115200 baud, then press the board's EN button to see its boot banner. On Linux: tio -b 115200 /dev/ttyACM0 The port may also be /dev/ttyUSB0. On Windows it is a COM port (COM3, …) in Device Manager; on macOS it is /dev/cu.usbserial-….
  3. 3
    The boot banner prints it as board MAC: 24:0A:C4:12:34:56. Colons, dashes or none, any case.

Your points and hints stay in this browser, per board. The board keeps its own score on its console (status), separate from this page.
